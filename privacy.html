What the app stores on your phone

Your puzzle progress (levels finished, stars, best times and unfinished puzzles) and your chosen language are saved in your browser's local storage on your phone. This data does not leave your phone unless you sign in.

Signing in (optional)

If you choose "Sign in with Google", the app uses Google Firebase Authentication. We receive your Google account name, email address, profile photo link and a user ID. Your puzzle progress is then saved in Google Cloud Firestore under your user ID so you can continue on another phone. Only you can read or change your saved progress. You can sign out at any time. To delete your account data, contact us at the email below.

Advertising

The app may show ads from Google AdSense on the home screen and after a level is finished. Google and its partners may use cookies or device identifiers to show ads and measure their performance. You can learn more and control ad personalisation at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Children

The app is a general-audience word game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what is described above.
